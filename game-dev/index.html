Grimoire

A Storyteller's companion for Blood on the Clocktower, styled as a late-medieval book. The rules of Trouble Brewing are kept by the app; the Storyteller runs the night from one phone, or links every player's phone so roles, night choices and announcements arrive privately.

Open it on a phone and choose Add to Home Screen to install. Players join a game with the Storyteller's four-letter code.
