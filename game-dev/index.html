Grimoire play

A Storyteller's companion for Blood on the Clocktower, styled as a late-medieval book. The app keeps the rules of Trouble Brewing, Bad Moon Rising and Sects & Violets, with Travellers, Fabled and custom scripts. The Storyteller runs the night from one phone, or links every player's phone so roles, night choices, nominations and votes arrive privately.

The Village: walk a fog-bound fifteenth-century hamlet (church and graveyard, tavern, smithy, market cross) on your own phone and talk to whoever is near you, voices fading with distance and stopping at walls; the town gathers in a ring at dusk and sleeps behind locked doors at night.

Open it on a phone and choose Add to Home Screen to install. Players join with the Storyteller's four-letter code. Nothing to sign up for; phones talk to each other directly.
